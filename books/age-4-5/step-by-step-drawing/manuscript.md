## Production notes

- Every multi-step sequence highlights the *newly added* element in the
  book's designated step-highlight color (defined in the image prompts),
  while previously drawn parts stay in outline/neutral color — this keeps
  sequencing legible for 4-5 year olds.
- No subject uses more than 6 total steps; simpler subjects (cat, house) use
  4, the rocket uses 4 shown across 3 pages for pacing variety.
- Keep numeral style identical across all step badges (same font, same badge
  shape/color) for visual consistency.
