## Production notes

- Every interior spread (2-11) uses exactly one dominant shape or pattern —
  no competing visual elements, per infant attention research.
- Reserve red only for pages 1, 7, and 10 to keep the two-color (b/w) to
  three-color (b/w/red) transition legible and not overwhelming.
- All shapes must remain fully inside the safe area defined in
  `templates/print-style.css` (no bleed-critical details).
