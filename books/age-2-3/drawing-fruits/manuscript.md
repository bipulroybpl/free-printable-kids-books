# Manuscript: My First Coloring Book: Fruits (Ages 2-3)

Total pages: 10 (cover + 8 fruit pages + back cover). Every fruit page is a
single bold black-line outline drawing on a plain white background, meant
to be colored in with crayons. No pre-filled color, no fine internal detail.

| Page | Text Content | Visual Description | Layout Type |
|------|-------------|---------------------|-------------|
| 1 (Cover) | "My First Coloring Book: Fruits" | Colorful sample of a few colored-in fruit outlines, bright background | Full-bleed, title top |
| 2 | "Color the apple" | Single bold black-line outline of an apple with a leaf, on white background, no internal detail | Full-page coloring outline |
| 3 | "Color the banana" | Single bold black-line outline of a banana, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 4 | "Color the orange" | Single bold black-line outline of an orange with a leaf, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 5 | "Color the grapes" | Single bold black-line outline of a simple grape bunch (large clustered shape, not individual tiny grapes), on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 6 | "Color the strawberry" | Single bold black-line outline of a strawberry with a leaf top, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 7 | "Color the watermelon slice" | Single bold black-line outline of a watermelon slice, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 8 | "Color the pineapple" | Single bold black-line outline of a pineapple, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 9 | "Color the pear" | Single bold black-line outline of a pear, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 10 (Back cover) | "The End — Great coloring!" | Small grid of all 8 fruit outlines in miniature | Centered text, bordered grid |

## Production notes

- Every outline must be large, bold, and free of small enclosed regions
  (e.g. avoid depicting individual visible seeds as separate tiny closed
  shapes) so a 2-3 year old can actually color inside the lines.
- No color should be pre-filled in the generated art -- these are coloring
  pages, not finished illustrations.
