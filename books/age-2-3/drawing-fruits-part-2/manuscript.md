# Manuscript: My First Coloring Book: Fruits (Part 2) (Ages 2-3)

Total pages: 10 (cover + 8 fruit pages + back cover). Continues the fruit
vocabulary set from Part 1 with 8 new fruits, same bold-outline coloring
format. No sequencing dependency on Part 1 -- can be used in either order.

| Page | Text Content | Visual Description | Layout Type |
|------|-------------|---------------------|-------------|
| 1 (Cover) | "My First Coloring Book: Fruits (Part 2)" | Colorful sample of a few colored-in fruit outlines, bright background | Full-bleed, title top |
| 2 | "Color the mango" | Single bold black-line outline of a mango with a leaf, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 3 | "Color the lemon" | Single bold black-line outline of a lemon, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 4 | "Color the peach" | Single bold black-line outline of a peach with a leaf, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 5 | "Color the cherries" | Single bold black-line outline of two cherries joined by stems, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 6 | "Color the plum" | Single bold black-line outline of a plum with a stem, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 7 | "Color the kiwi" | Single bold black-line outline of a round kiwi fruit, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 8 | "Color the coconut" | Single bold black-line outline of a coconut, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 9 | "Color the fig" | Single bold black-line outline of a fig with a stem, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 10 (Back cover) | "The End — Great coloring!" | Small grid of all 8 fruit outlines in miniature | Centered text, bordered grid |

## Production notes

- Same production rules as Part 1: large, bold, no small enclosed regions,
  no pre-filled color.
- Cherries page is the only two-object page in the set (a natural pair,
  common depiction), kept large and simple with clear separation.
