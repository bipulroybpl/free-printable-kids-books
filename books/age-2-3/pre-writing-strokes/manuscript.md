# Manuscript: My First Pre-Writing Strokes (Ages 2-3)

Total pages: 10 (cover + 8 stroke pages + back cover). Every page is
generated programmatically (not via AI image generation) as a large gray
traceable geometric stroke -- see `prompts/tracing_config.json` and
`.github/workflows/scripts/generate_tracing_pages.py`.

**Developmental note:** this is the most age-appropriate "first tracing"
book in the series. Occupational-therapy guidance places these 8 strokes,
in roughly this order, as the actual precursor skills to letter writing,
typically emerging between ages 2 and 3.5 -- well before letterform tracing
(see `drawing-abc-letters`, which is a later/parallel-track book for this
same age band, done hand-over-hand with a caregiver).

## Production notes

- Strokes are ordered by typical developmental emergence: straight lines
  first, circle and cross next, diagonal/curve/zigzag/square last.
- All 8 pages are rendered by `generate_tracing_pages.py`, not generated by
  an AI image model, for the same precision reasons as the ABC/123 tracing
  books.
