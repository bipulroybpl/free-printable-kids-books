# Manuscript: My First Coloring Book: Animals (Part 2) (Ages 2-3)

Total pages: 10 (cover + 8 animal pages + back cover). Continues the animal
vocabulary set from Part 1 with 8 new animals, same bold-outline coloring
format. No sequencing dependency on Part 1.

| Page | Text Content | Visual Description | Layout Type |
|------|-------------|---------------------|-------------|
| 1 (Cover) | "My First Coloring Book: Animals (Part 2)" | Colorful sample of a few colored-in animal outlines, bright background | Full-bleed, title top |
| 2 | "Color the sheep" | Single bold black-line outline of a friendly standing sheep, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 3 | "Color the pig" | Single bold black-line outline of a friendly standing pig, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 4 | "Color the horse" | Single bold black-line outline of a friendly standing horse, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 5 | "Color the frog" | Single bold black-line outline of a friendly sitting frog, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 6 | "Color the fox" | Single bold black-line outline of a friendly sitting fox, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 7 | "Color the deer" | Single bold black-line outline of a friendly standing deer with antlers, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 8 | "Color the bear" | Single bold black-line outline of a friendly standing bear cub, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 9 | "Color the turtle" | Single bold black-line outline of a friendly turtle with a shell, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 10 (Back cover) | "The End — Great coloring!" | Small grid of all 8 outlines in miniature | Centered text, bordered grid |

## Production notes

- Same production rules as Part 1: large, bold, no small enclosed regions,
  no pre-filled color.
- Bear depicted as a calm, standing cub -- no roaring/aggressive pose,
  consistent with the safety notes carried through this series.
