# Manuscript: My First 123 Tracing (Ages 2-3)

Total pages: 12 (cover + 10 numeral pages + back cover). Every numeral page
is generated programmatically (not via AI image generation) as a large gray
traceable numeral with a small solid black reference badge in the top
corner -- see `prompts/tracing_config.json` and
`.github/workflows/scripts/generate_tracing_pages.py`.

**Developmental note:** at ages 2-3, this book is meant for **hand-over-hand
tracing with a caregiver**, not independent correct-stroke handwriting.
