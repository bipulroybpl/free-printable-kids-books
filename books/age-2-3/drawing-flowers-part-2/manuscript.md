# Manuscript: My First Coloring Book: Flowers (Part 2) (Ages 2-3)

Total pages: 10 (cover + 8 pages + back cover). Continues the flower/nature
vocabulary set from Part 1 with 8 new items, same bold-outline coloring
format. No sequencing dependency on Part 1.

| Page | Text Content | Visual Description | Layout Type |
|------|-------------|---------------------|-------------|
| 1 (Cover) | "My First Coloring Book: Flowers (Part 2)" | Colorful sample of a few colored-in outlines, bright background | Full-bleed, title top |
| 2 | "Color the lily" | Single bold black-line outline of a lily with long simple petals, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 3 | "Color the hibiscus" | Single bold black-line outline of a large simple hibiscus flower, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 4 | "Color the cactus" | Single bold black-line outline of a simple rounded cactus with two arm bumps, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 5 | "Color the acorn" | Single bold black-line outline of an acorn with a cap, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 6 | "Color the cloud" | Single bold black-line outline of a simple fluffy cloud, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 7 | "Color the rainbow" | Single bold black-line outline of a simple arched rainbow (bands as outlines), on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 8 | "Color the star" | Single bold black-line outline of a simple five-point star, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 9 | "Color the bush" | Single bold black-line outline of a simple rounded bush, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 10 (Back cover) | "The End — Great coloring!" | Small grid of all 8 outlines in miniature | Centered text, bordered grid |

## Production notes

- Same production rules as Part 1: large, bold, no small enclosed regions,
  no pre-filled color.
- Cactus explicitly has NO spines/needles depicted (safety: avoid anything
  suggesting a sharp/prickly object for this age), just a friendly rounded
  cactus silhouette.
