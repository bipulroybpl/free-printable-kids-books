# Manuscript: My First ABC Tracing (Ages 2-3)

Total pages: 28 (cover + 26 letter pages + back cover). Every letter page is
generated programmatically (not via AI image generation) as a large gray
traceable letterform with a small solid black reference badge in the top
corner -- see `prompts/tracing_config.json` and
`.github/workflows/scripts/generate_tracing_pages.py`.

**Developmental note:** at ages 2-3, this book is meant for **hand-over-hand
tracing with a caregiver** (finger or a chunky crayon), not independent
correct-stroke handwriting. Keep expectations to "trace together," not
"trace alone correctly."

## Production notes

- All 26 letter pages are rendered by `generate_tracing_pages.py`, not
  generated by an AI image model -- this content type needs pixel-precise
  typography, which AI generators are unreliable at (see the ABC
  illustration book's failed split-panel attempt for why this book avoids
  the same approach).
- Cover and back cover pages are the only pages needing an illustrated/AI
  element (or can remain simple text-only per `render_pdf.py`'s default
  text-only rendering for pages with no matching image file).
