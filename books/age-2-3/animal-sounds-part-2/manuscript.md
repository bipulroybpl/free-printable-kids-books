## Production notes

- Every animal must be rendered as a single friendly character alone in its
  scene -- no other animals, no human characters, no props beyond a simple
  one-element setting implied by background color only, per the lesson
  learned from the ABC book's failed split-panel attempt.
- Bear page carries the explicit safety framing: a smiling, sitting cub,
  never a roaring/aggressive adult pose.
- Fox, deer, camel, and turtle "sounds" are less iconic than farm animals --
  kept intentionally soft/gentle in the text rather than forcing an
  unnatural loud sound word.
