# Manuscript: My First Animal Sounds (Ages 2-3)

Total pages: 14 (cover + 12 animal pages + back cover). Every animal page
follows the same simple single-scene layout, deliberately avoiding the
split-panel / multi-element composition mistakes made on the ABC book: one
friendly cartoon animal, one clear scene, one sound word, full-bleed
illustration with a text banner at the bottom.

Text follows a consistent call-and-response pattern: "The [animal] says...
[Sound]!" -- read aloud, a caregiver can pause before the sound to invite the
child to guess or imitate it.

## Production notes

- Every animal must be rendered as a single friendly character alone in its
  scene -- no other animals, no human characters, no props beyond a simple
  one-element setting (field, pond, branch, flower) implied by background
  color/shape only, keeping generation complexity low per the lesson learned
  from the ABC book's failed split-panel attempt.
- The lion and bee pages carry the two safety-sensitive substitutions: lion
  as a smiling cub (not a roaring adult), bee as a rounded non-threatening
  shape (not a realistic wasp-like render).
- Sound words are capitalized and exclamation-marked consistently across all
  12 pages to support the read-aloud rhythm.
