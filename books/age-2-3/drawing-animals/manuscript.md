# Manuscript: My First Coloring Book: Animals (Ages 2-3)

Total pages: 10 (cover + 8 animal pages + back cover). Every animal page is a
single bold black-line outline drawing on a plain white background, meant to
be colored in with crayons. No pre-filled color, no fine internal detail.
Pairs with the fully-illustrated `animal-sounds` book as a coloring
companion using the same familiar animals.

## Production notes

- Every outline must be large, bold, and free of small enclosed regions
  (e.g. avoid fine fur texture lines) so a 2-3 year old can actually color
  inside the lines.
- All animals depicted in calm, friendly, standing/sitting poses -- no
  aggressive or predatory poses, consistent with the safety notes in
  `animal-sounds`.
