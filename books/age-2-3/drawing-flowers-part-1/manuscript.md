# Manuscript: My First Coloring Book: Flowers (Ages 2-3)

Total pages: 10 (cover + 8 flower pages + back cover). Every flower page is a
single bold black-line outline drawing on a plain white background, meant
to be colored in with crayons. No pre-filled color, no fine internal detail.

| Page | Text Content | Visual Description | Layout Type |
|------|-------------|---------------------|-------------|
| 1 (Cover) | "My First Coloring Book: Flowers" | Colorful sample of a few colored-in flower outlines, bright background | Full-bleed, title top |
| 2 | "Color the sunflower" | Single bold black-line outline of a large simple sunflower, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 3 | "Color the tulip" | Single bold black-line outline of a tulip with a stem and one leaf, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 4 | "Color the daisy" | Single bold black-line outline of a simple daisy with a large center, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 5 | "Color the rose" | Single bold black-line outline of a simplified rose shape (large simple swirl, not fine petal detail), on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 6 | "Color the tree" | Single bold black-line outline of a simple round-top tree, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 7 | "Color the butterfly" | Single bold black-line outline of a simple symmetric butterfly, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 8 | "Color the leaf" | Single bold black-line outline of a large simple leaf, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 9 | "Color the mushroom" | Single bold black-line outline of a simple rounded mushroom, on white background | Full-page coloring outline |
| 10 (Back cover) | "The End — Great coloring!" | Small grid of all 8 outlines in miniature | Centered text, bordered grid |

## Production notes

- Every outline must be large, bold, and free of small enclosed regions
  (e.g. simplify the rose/sunflower to one large swirl/round shape rather
  than many tiny individual petals) so a 2-3 year old can actually color
  inside the lines.
- Butterfly included as a nature-adjacent, safe, universally appealing
  addition to round out the set at 8 pages.
