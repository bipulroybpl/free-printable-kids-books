## Production notes

- Every "difficult" emotion (sad, angry, scared) must be followed within 1-2
  pages by a resolution/coping page — never leave the character in distress.
- Maintain one consistent character model sheet across the book (proportions,
  color, facial structure) — vary only pose and expression.
- Avoid crying with excessive tears, gritted teeth, or wide-open screaming
  mouths; keep all expressions soft and age-appropriate.
