# Manuscript: Baby's First Learning ABC (Ages 2-3)

Total pages: 28 (cover + 26 letter pages + back cover). Every letter page uses
the same split-panel layout: a glossy 3D letter with a cute face on the left
(light neutral background), and a glossy 3D object starting with that letter
with a matching cute face on the right (bright single-color background),
separated by a thin vertical divider — matching the reference style.

**Word choices are deliberately the safest, most globally-recognizable option
per letter.** Two substitutions were made specifically for child safety:
- **K → Kite** (not Knife — avoids depicting a sharp/bladed object)
- **X → Xylophone** (no natural safe/common X object for toddlers; avoids
  X-ray or other inappropriate/ambiguous options)

## Production notes

- The left-panel letter treatment (glossy 3D, googly cartoon eyes, same smile
  style, same light neutral background) must stay pixel-consistent across all
  26 pages — only the letterform itself changes.
- The right-panel object background color rotates through the `primary`
  palette (red, blue, yellow, green) in sequence across pages purely for
  visual variety; the object and its face style stay consistent in rendering
  technique (glossy 3D or friendly cartoon, matching the reference image).
- No object is depicted at a scale or with details suggesting it's a graspable
  small toy with detachable parts (relevant for grapes, ice cream, jam jar).
